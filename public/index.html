How it works

From a link to a working copy in three steps

  1. 1

    Paste the address

    Pick a mode and how many pages to save. Nothing to install, no settings to learn.

  2. 2

    Links are rewritten to local files. Unzip it and open the site — offline, on any computer.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Create a free account and try it on your own site.

Can it download a website's backend code?

No. Server code and databases never leave the server. You get everything a browser receives — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and images — including the HTML of signed-in screens when you use the Behind a login mode.

Does it work with React, Next.js or Framer sites?

Yes. Pages are opened in a real Chromium browser and saved after their JavaScript has rendered. Interactive parts like sliders and 3D run when you open the copy with the included launcher.

How do I save pages behind a login?

Sign in to the site in your own browser, export its cookies as JSON with the free Cookie-Editor extension, and paste them into the Behind a login mode. If the site keeps its login in local storage, the form gives you a one-line command for that too. Your cookies are used once and never stored. Only do this for accounts you own or may use.

Why is there an "Open website.bat" file in the ZIP?

Browsers block JavaScript modules, data loading and many 3D textures on files opened straight from your disk. The launcher serves the folder from your own computer at localhost, so the copy behaves exactly like the real site. Opening index.html directly still works for looking through the pages.

How long are my ZIP files kept?

24 hours, then they are deleted automatically. Your download history stays in your dashboard, and you can run a download again at any time.

Which websites am I allowed to download?

Your own sites, sites you have permission for, and public pages for personal reference and learning. Don't republish other people's content or designs as your own.
