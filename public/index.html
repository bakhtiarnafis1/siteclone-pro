How it works

Three steps. No setup.

01

Paste a link

Pick one page, a whole site (up to 50 pages), or pages you can only see after logging in.

02

We capture it

A real browser loads each page, scrolls to the bottom, and saves everything it renders, including JavaScript-built content.

03

Download the ZIP

Links are rewritten to local files, so you can unzip it, open index.html, and it just works.

Questions

Can it download a site's backend code?

No. Server code and databases never leave the server. You get everything the browser receives: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, fonts and images.

Does it work with React, Next.js or Framer sites?

Yes. Pages are opened in a real Chromium browser and captured after the JavaScript has finished rendering.

How do I capture pages behind a login?

Log in to the site in your own browser, export its cookies as JSON with the free Cookie-Editor extension, and paste them into the "Behind login" tab. Only do this for accounts you own or are allowed to use.

Which sites am I allowed to clone?

Your own sites, sites you have permission for, and public pages for personal reference and learning. Don't republish other people's content or designs as your own.
